# Sample Inputs

## 1. Lonely Robot

```text
A lonely robot wakes up in an abandoned railway station and tries to find where the last train went.
```

Expected characteristics:

- 4 to 6 scenes
- Lonely or emotional tone
- Robot remains the central character
- Abandoned railway station and train mystery remain visually continuous
- Ending can be hopeful or unresolved, but should stay grounded in the story

## 2. Glowing Seed

```text
A village girl discovers a glowing seed during a drought and must decide whether to plant it or sell it.
```

Expected characteristics:

- Moral conflict
- Village and drought setting
- Emotional decision around planting or selling
- Hopeful ending
- No unrelated magic system or extra villain is needed

## 3. Mars Signal

```text
Two astronauts on Mars receive a strange signal from beneath the ground.
```

Expected characteristics:

- Sci-fi tone
- Mars environment
- Signal investigation
- Suspenseful reveal
- Two astronauts remain visually continuous across scenes
